Method

How to read this dashboard

Start with sectors, never with a stock. Money flows into sectors first and stocks follow.

1 · Scan the Top 25Which sectors are new? What segment are they in? Skip groups with 1–3 stocks.
2 · Track the moversNote 3–5 groups with a clearly improving journey (Emerging leaders).
3 · Open those sectorsKeep only A/B stocks with EPS 80+, price strength 80+ and buyer demand B+ or better.
4 · Build a watchlistEnd with 5–15 names and take each through fundamental research. Candidates, not recommendations.
5 · Repeat weeklySectors and stocks that keep showing up (Conviction) are the highest-conviction zone.

Reading a stock

MasterScore A–EOne-letter verdict on earnings, price strength and demand. A/B strong, C average, D/E weak.
EPS rank 1–99Earnings growth vs all listed companies. 80+ is elite, under 50 is mediocre.
Price strength 0–99Price performance vs every other stock. 80+ beats 80% of the market.
Buyer demand A+ to EBuying vs selling pressure. A = heavy accumulation, C neutral, D/E distribution.

Price without earnings: high price strength with a low EPS rank is often speculation. Sometimes the market is pricing earnings still to come, so a low EPS rank needs a stronger fundamental case, not an automatic rejection.

Scores and segments

Momentum Score = 40% current strength (groups + 1 − current rank) + 35% ranks gained vs 3 months ago + 15% vs 6 months ago + 10% vs last week. MRank sorts that score, ties broken by the better current rank.

Established Leader top 45 in all four periods. Emerging Leader top 45 now and 60+ ranks better than 6 months ago. Turnaround Candidate 120 or worse 3 and 6 months ago, 15+ ranks better than 3 months ago and still climbing weekly. Fading Sector top 45 at some point in the last 6 months, slipped 40+ ranks since. Everything else is Rangebound. Thin groups (3 or fewer stocks) are hidden unless you untick "Ignore thin groups" on the All sectors tab.

The sheet is read on your device; an uploaded file is never sent anywhere. This is a price-momentum screen for education, not investment advice.

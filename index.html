How this is calculated

Momentum Score = 40% current strength (groups + 1 − current rank) + 35% ranks gained vs 3 months ago + 15% vs 6 months ago + 10% vs last week. MRank sorts that score, ties broken by the better current rank. The page recomputes this from the four rank columns, so it works on the raw NovaWealth file too.

Segments, checked in this order: Established Leader top 45 in all four periods; Emerging Leader top 45 now and 60+ ranks better than 6 months ago; Turnaround Candidate ranked 120 or worse 3 and 6 months ago, 15+ ranks better than 3 months ago and still climbing this week; Fading Sector was top 45 at some point in the last 6 months and has slipped 40+ ranks since; everything else is Rangebound.

Reading a stock

MasterScore A–EOne-letter verdict combining earnings, price strength and demand. A/B strong, C average, D/E weak.
EPS rank 1–99Earnings growth vs all listed companies. 80+ is elite, under 50 is mediocre.
Price strength 0–99Price performance vs every other stock. 80+ means it is beating 80% of the market.
Buyer demand A+ to EBuying vs selling pressure. A grades = heavy accumulation, C neutral, D/E distribution.

The strong-stock rule used in Stock picks and Conviction: MasterScore A or B, EPS rank 80+, price strength 80+ and buyer demand B+ or better, inside a top-ranked sector. Cells are shaded green at 80+ or A grades, amber for C and red for D/E or under 50.

Price without earnings: high price strength with a low EPS rank is often speculation. Sometimes the market is pricing earnings that have not arrived yet, so a low EPS rank needs a stronger fundamental case rather than an automatic rejection.

Weekly routine: start with sectors, never with a stock. Scan the Top 25 for new names; note 3–5 groups with a clearly improving journey; open those sectors and keep only A/B stocks that pass the rule; build a 5–15 name watchlist for fundamental work; and favour sectors and stocks that keep showing up week after week (Conviction tab).

Thin groups have 3 or fewer stocks; one stock can move their rank, so they are hidden unless you untick "Ignore thin groups".

The file is read in your browser and is not uploaded anywhere. Your browser keeps a small summary of each week you load so it can show what changed since the last upload. This is a price-momentum screen for education, not investment advice.
